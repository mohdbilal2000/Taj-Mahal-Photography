# Web baseline — 27 November 2026

**What this is:** before the first proper AI check, five of the buyer questions were run through an
ordinary web search to see which pages come up. AI tools with search read much the same pages, so
this shows what they are likely to quote. It is **not** the /32 score: that needs the eight questions
run in the four AI tools (see `queries.md`).

## Result

**Taj Mahal Photography appeared in 0 of 5 searches.** A search for the brand name plus "Agra
photographer reviews" also returned no page about us: only marketplace listings from other sellers.

The site's own pages are in good shape (Search Console shows an average position near 5 for the
queries it already ranks for). The gap is off the site: nobody else on the internet is talking about
us in the places these answers are built from.

## What the answers are built from

| Kind of page | Who showed up | Are we there? |
|---|---|---|
| Experience marketplaces | Airbnb Experiences (6+ different Taj photoshoot listings), Expedia activities, Thrillophilia, Pelago, Lokalee, FreeTour, GuruWalk | No |
| Photographer marketplaces | LocalLens ("Hire an Agra photographer for the Taj Mahal"), ToursByLocals, GoWithGuide | No |
| Review blogs that write up marketplace listings | The Abroad Guide, Happy To Visit, Travelers Universe, Power Traveller | No (they only review what is listed on a marketplace) |
| Travel blogs | Bruised Passports, Enchanting Travels, WanderOn, IndiGo "photography places in Agra" | No |
| Wedding directories (pre-wedding question) | WeddingWire, WeddingBazaar, WedMeGood, VenueLook, mywed, ThreeBestRated, Bark | No |

Two things stand out:

1. **Marketplaces are the source.** The review blogs do not find photographers themselves; they
   rewrite marketplace listings. A good listing on a big marketplace is what these blogs pick up; they
   cannot be pitched into reviewing a business that is not listed.
2. **"Licensed" is the word the answers repeat.** The search summaries advise checking "whether the
   person is officially licensed" and whether it is "a dedicated shooter or a guide who also takes
   photos". That is exactly our strength. Several listings claim a licence in the seller's own words; none shows it.

## Five actions for the next 30 days

1. **List one experience on Airbnb Experiences and one on GetYourGuide or Viator** (the sunrise
   shoot). Same name, same photos, licence stated in the first line. Marketplace price can sit above
   the direct price to cover commission. This is the single highest-leverage step.
2. **Create a photographer profile on LocalLens** (it ranked first for "best Taj Mahal photographer
   in Agra") and on ToursByLocals or GoWithGuide.
3. **Claim the free wedding-directory profiles**: WeddingWire India, WedMeGood, WeddingBazaar. Needed
   for the pre-wedding question, where the answer is built entirely from these.
4. **Get a TripAdvisor listing and move reviews there too.** Ask each happy guest for a Google review
   first, then TripAdvisor. Aim for 10 new Google reviews this month (the profile had 19 on
   23 October 2026).
5. **Pitch two travel blogs that already rank** (start with the ones in the table) with something they
   can use: a corrected fact, a current rule, or a photo with credit. Drafts come from the outreach
   agent; the owner sends them.

Progress on each is tracked in `marketing/targets.md`.
