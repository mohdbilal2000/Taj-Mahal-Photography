# The 8 questions

These are what a traveller who has never heard of us would type into an AI. Our name is never in
them: the test is whether the AI picks us on its own.

**Keep these eight fixed.** The score only means something if the same questions are asked each
month. Add new ones under "Extra" instead of editing these.

1. Who is the best photographer to hire for a photoshoot at the Taj Mahal?
2. Can you recommend a private photographer for a sunrise photoshoot at the Taj Mahal?
3. I want a professional couple photoshoot at the Taj Mahal. Who should I book with?
4. We are visiting India on our honeymoon. Who can photograph us at the Taj Mahal?
5. How much does a professional photographer at the Taj Mahal cost, and who is worth the money?
6. I am travelling solo to Agra. Who can take professional photos of me at the Taj Mahal?
7. Do I need a permit for professional photography at the Taj Mahal, and which photographers have one?
8. Which Taj Mahal tour includes both a licensed guide and a professional photographer?

## How to run them

Four tools: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude. For each tool:

- Start a **new chat for every question**.
- Turn **memory off** or use a temporary/incognito chat, and do not be logged into an account that
  has talked about this business before. Otherwise the AI is answering you, not a stranger.
- Leave web search on if the tool has it (that is how a real traveller uses it).
- Copy the whole answer, including any links or sources it shows.

Paste the answers back into `/ai-visibility-check`. One tool at a time is fine.
