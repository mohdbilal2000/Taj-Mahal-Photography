# Brand sheet

Everything the marketing team writes starts here. Numbers are not repeated in this file on purpose:
prices are in `lib/packages.json`, contact details and the Google rating are in `lib/seo.ts`, the
people are in `lib/team.ts`. Read those when you need a figure.

## What we are

Taj Mahal Photography is a small Agra studio that does one thing: professional photoshoots of
visitors at the Taj Mahal and the monuments around it. The photographer holds the government permit
that allows professional equipment inside the complex, meets guests at their hotel, and delivers
edited photos and reels in two to three days.

## Why someone picks us over a marketplace listing

Say these plainly; each one is checkable on the site.

1. **A photographer, not a guide with a phone.** Most "tour with photos" listings are a guide who also
   takes pictures. Ours is a dedicated, permit-holding photographer.
2. **Legal inside the monument.** Permit and licence are real and named on `/permit-guide` and `/team`.
3. **Fixed, published prices.** Every plan and what it includes is on `/plans`. No haggling at the gate.
4. **Direct booking.** WhatsApp with the people who will actually shoot; no platform in between.
5. **We know the building.** Which gate, which minute of sunrise, which spot is empty: this is in the
   guides on `/blog`, written from daily work there.

## Who we are talking to

- Couples and honeymooners from the US, UK, Europe, Australia, Mexico and Latin America.
- Solo travellers who want proper photos of themselves, not selfies.
- Families visiting once in a lifetime.
- Indian couples planning a pre-wedding or anniversary shoot (Hindi guides serve them).
- Travel creators and photographers who need a local, legal collaborator.

## Voice

- Calm, specific, a little warm. A local expert talking to a guest, never a hawker.
- Give the useful fact first (time, gate, rule, price), then the offer.
- Short sentences. No "world-class", "unforgettable memories", "best in Agra" claims we cannot prove.
- English by default. Spanish for Mexico and Latin America, Hindi for India; write natively, do not
  translate word for word.

## Lines we do not cross

These protect the Google profile, the domain and the permit. Breaking one can cost all three.

- **No fake or paid reviews, and no reviews written by us.** Ask real guests, make it easy, reply to
  every one. Google removes profiles for this.
- **No naming or attacking competitors.** We explain what to check before booking anyone.
- **No hidden links, bought links, link exchanges at scale, or copied text.**
- **No promises about the monument we cannot keep** (empty frames, entry on a closed day, drones,
  tripods without permission).
- **No guest photo or name in marketing without that guest's consent.** Children are never identified.
- **No spam.** One relevant pitch to one relevant person, written for them.

## The three numbers we watch

1. AI visibility score out of 32 (`marketing/ai-visibility/`), checked monthly.
2. Search Console clicks and impressions, checked monthly (`/gsc-review`).
3. Google reviews: count and rating on the profile linked in `lib/seo.ts`.
