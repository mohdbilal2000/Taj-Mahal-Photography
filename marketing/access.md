# Access — team ko kya chahiye

Team khud kahin login nahi karti aur password kabhi nahi maangti. Neeche har cheez ke saath likha hai
woh kya kholti hai aur aap kaise dete ho. Jo ho jaye, `have` likh do (ya Claude ko bol do).

**Password, OTP ya API key chat mein kabhi paste mat karo.**

| Cheez | Status | Isse kya hoga | Kaise dena hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub repo (site ka code) | have | Guides likhna aur publish karna | Ho chuka |
| Vercel (deploy) | have | `main` par push hote hi site live | Ho chuka |
| Google Search Console export | have (manual) | Kaunsi query par kaunsa page, kya index nahi hua | Mahine mein ek baar Performance + Pages ki Excel upload karo |
| Google Business Profile | owner ke paas | Hafte ka post, review replies | Team text deti hai, aap paste karte ho. Naye reviews paste kar do |
| Instagram / Facebook | owner ke paas | Hafte ke 5 posts | Team captions deti hai, aap post karte ho |
| Reddit account (apne asli naam/business se) | need | Sawaalon ke jawab. Perplexity aur Google ke AI jawab Reddit padhte hain; local business ke liye asar chhota hai | Account banao, profile mein likho aap kaun ho. Username yahan likh do. Jawab aap khud post karoge |
| Quora account | need | Wahi, Quora par | Account banao, username yahan likh do |
| LinkedIn (photographer ke naam se) | have | Har guide ka LinkedIn version | Team text deti hai, aap post karte ho |
| Medium ya Substack | need | Har guide ka doosra version, original ka link ke saath | Free account banao, username yahan likh do |
| YouTube channel | need | Shorts jisme business ka naam ho; research mein AI visibility se sabse zyada juda factor yahi tha | Channel banao, link yahan likh do |
| Pinterest business account | need | Photos se pins; travel planning ke liye kaam ka | Account banao, link yahan likh do |
| TripAdvisor listing | need | Reviews jo foreign travellers aur AI dono dekhte hain | `marketing/targets.md` dekho; team steps degi |
| Marketplace accounts (Airbnb Experiences, GetYourGuide, Viator, LocalLens) | need | Sabse bada gap: AI inhi ko padh raha hai | Ek-ek karke; bolo "Airbnb listing taiyaar kar" |
| Bing Places (Bing ka business profile) | need | Local sawaalon mein ChatGPT Bing ke business data par tikta hai | bingplaces.com par Google Business Profile se import karo |
| ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity apps | owner ke paas | Mahine ka /32 score | `/ai-visibility-check` chalao aur jawab paste karo |
| Bing Webmaster Tools | need | ChatGPT ke jawab Google ke saath Bing ki ranking se bhi jude hain; yahan site verify honi chahiye | bing.com/webmasters par Google Search Console se import karo (2 minute) |

## Team ke sawaal (aapke jawab ka intezaar)

- Jin guests ki photos site par hain, unme se kinhone social media par use ki ijaazat di hai?
- Har hafte kitne shoots hote hain (bas andaaza)? Isse review ka target banega.
