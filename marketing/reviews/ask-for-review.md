# Review maangne ka message

**Kab bhejna hai:** jis message mein photos deliver karte ho, uske turant baad. Us waqt guest sabse
khush hota hai. Har asli guest ko bhejo; kisi ke liye review khud kabhi mat likho, aur review ke
badle discount ya gift mat do (Google profile hata sakta hai).

Link: `https://maps.google.com/?cid=9101268072152291089` (yahi `lib/seo.ts` mein hai). Profile khulne
par guest "Write a review" dabata hai. `[Name]` ki jagah guest ka naam likho.

## English

> Hi [Name], your photos are all yours now. I hope you love them as much as we enjoyed the morning.
>
> One small favour: a short Google review helps other travellers find a licensed photographer here.
> What was the shoot like, and would you tell a friend to book?
> https://maps.google.com/?cid=9101268072152291089
>
> Thank you, and safe travels.
